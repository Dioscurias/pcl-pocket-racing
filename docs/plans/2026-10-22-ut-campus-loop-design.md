# UT Campus Loop Design

## Goal

Replace the existing UT Grand Prix route with the supplied campus loop while retaining PCL Lobby Loop, Bookstack Blitz, and Jester Flight as separate courses.

## Route

The course begins on Speedway by the start line, travels north to Dean Keeton, turns south along San Jacinto, crosses west on 23rd Street, runs south and west around DKR Stadium, follows 21st Street to Guadalupe, turns north to 24th Street, then returns through University Avenue and Inner Campus Drive to Speedway.

The layout follows the user-provided reference image in order and proportion rather than the previous fictional PCL-to-Jester path. It remains a closed, one-way circuit with wide arcade corners and soft rails.

## Environment

The outdoor scene uses a saturated toy-scale campus treatment: bright grass, shaded sidewalks, clear road markings, and a readable black asphalt route. The UT Tower, PCL, DKR Stadium, LBJ, McCombs, Gregory Gym, Waller Creek, and large campus trees appear at their referenced positions around the loop.

The route has no airborne Jester section, indoor floor, or kite mechanic. Existing boost pads, item boxes, checkpoints, guard rails, camera, physics, multiplayer, and HUD continue to operate against the replacement track data.

## Safety and Test Coverage

The route has a wide consistent track width, rounded control points, full floor boundary, and retained soft rails. Automated tests will verify its landmark order, loop closure, driveable width, and removal of obsolete airborne-Jester course data.
