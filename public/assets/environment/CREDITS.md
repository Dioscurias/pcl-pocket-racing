## Processing

The ambientCG 1K JPG packages were downloaded on 2026-10-24. Color, normal, roughness, and available ambient-occlusion maps were retained. Khronos `toktx` 4.4.2 generated mipmapped KTX2 files. Color and scalar textures use ETC1S. Normal maps use UASTC with Zstandard supercompression. The Sunflowers Pure Sky 1K HDR file is unchanged.

Tree leaf-card art and UT Grand Prix banner graphics are generated by the game at runtime and do not include third-party artwork.

The local Basis transcoder files are copied from the installed Three.js package. Three.js is MIT licensed and Basis Universal is Apache-2.0 licensed.

## Transfer size

Environment textures, HDRI, and the local Basis decoder total **12,601,679 bytes (12.02 MiB)** in the deployed public directory.
